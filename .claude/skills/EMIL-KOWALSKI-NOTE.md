# Skills from Emil Kowalski

Copied on 2026-10-05 from https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills (MIT license, kept in EMIL-KOWALSKI-LICENSE). Not edited. The 14 skills are
animate, animate-expo, animation-vocabulary, apple-design, ask-sonner, break-ui, emil-design-eng, find-animation-opportunities,
improve-animations, mobile-native, pick-ui-library, prototype, review-animations, write-swift. To update, copy the repo's skills folder over these.

This project's own rules win where they disagree (CLAUDE.md):
- Design tokens only: no new color, text size, radius, or weight that is not in design/tokens.json.
- No new libraries and no outside loads: the site's Content-Security-Policy allows only its own files. Do not take pick-ui-library or ask-sonner suggestions that add a package.
- Receipts, not scores; plain English; no dashes; no left accent stripes; no colored dots beside labels; both styles, both layouts, English and Spanish, light and dark.
- A fix lives in the source (ext/) or build.py, and every change passes scripts/release.py.

Where they help: apple-design, animate and mobile-native for touch, gestures, and sheets (the map and the story viewer); break-ui for long names and
empty states; review-animations before shipping motion. write-swift and animate-expo do not apply to this project.
